User Account & Data Deletion Requests

In compliance with Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v) and EU GDPR regulations, users may request deletion of account data associated with any SPNSS EOOD application:

  1. Email support@spnss.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request.
  2. Specify your account email or registered username in the application.
  3. Our privacy team will process and permanently remove your data within 30 days and send confirmation upon completion.
