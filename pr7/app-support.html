User Account & Data Deletion Requests

In compliance with Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v) and EU GDPR regulations, users have the right to request deletion of their account data associated with any SPNSS EOOD application.

  1. Send an email to support@spnss.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request.
  2. Include your account username or registered email address used in the app.
  3. Our privacy team will process and permanently remove your data within 30 calendar days and send confirmation upon completion.
