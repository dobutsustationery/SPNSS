Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 28, 2026 | Data Controller: SPNSS EOOD (Sofia, Bulgaria)

1. Overview & Data Controller

This Privacy Policy outlines how SPNSS EOOD ("Company", "we", "us", or "our"), a registered entity under Bulgarian law at 4 Hristo Belchev St., Floor 1, Sofia 1000, Bulgaria, collects, uses, and protects information gathered through our official corporate website and mobile applications published on the Apple App Store.

We are committed to safeguarding personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (EU 2016/679) and Apple Developer Program Guidelines.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Corporate Website Inquiries

When you submit a contact form or inquiry regarding our business consulting, translation, ESL services, or mobile applications, we collect your name, email address, inquiry category, and message content.

2.2 Mobile Applications (iOS)

Our mobile applications published on the Apple App Store are designed with privacy by default:

  • Account Data: If an app requires account creation, we collect your email address and display name.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Anonymous crash reports and performance metrics collected via Apple's native diagnostic frameworks to resolve software bugs.
  • In-App Purchases: All financial transactions are processed securely by Apple App Store billing. We do not store or process credit card details.

2.3 E-Commerce Operations

For purchases made via our Japanese stationery storefront (dobutsustationery.com), transactional data is governed by the store's dedicated checkout privacy terms.

3. Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal information under the following legal bases:

  • Contractual Necessity: To deliver requested consulting, translation, ESL services, or mobile app features.
  • Legitimate Interests: To maintain, secure, and optimize our corporate services and application stability.
  • Compliance with Legal Obligations: To comply with EU statutory accounting and tax regulations.

4. Data Retention & Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the operational purposes described in this policy, or as required by applicable Bulgarian tax and corporate laws.

5. User Rights Under GDPR & Apple Guidelines

Under EU Data Protection legislation and Apple App Store policies, you hold the following rights:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to Erasure (Account Deletion): Request complete deletion of your account and personal data.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request restrictions on processing your data.

To exercise any of these rights, please submit a request to privacy@spnss.com or visit our iOS App Support Portal.
